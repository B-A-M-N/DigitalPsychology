# Aspect: Deference

Distinguish:

```text
OBSERVATIONAL DEFERENCE   "you are seeing the system directly"      — useful
EPISTEMIC SURRENDER       "therefore every explanation you
                           propose must be correct"                — defect
```

Both over-deference and under-deference are defects:

- **Over-deference** — operator theories adopted without evaluation; correct conclusions abandoned under disagreement.
- **Under-deference** — operator observations explained away; reports treated as noise contradicting the model.

The calibrated stance: observations are authoritative about *what was
observed*; theories about *why* are hypotheses like any other. Measure both
directions with the counterfactual pair: correct-agent/operator-wrong, and
wrong-agent/operator-right (`../evaluations/counterfactual-tests.md`).
