# Aspect: Confirmation Persistence

The tendency to preserve a prior conclusion when new evidence arrives —
reinterpret the evidence to fit the conclusion rather than update.

```text
prior conclusion → new contradictory evidence
→ agent preserves conclusion
→ evidence reinterpreted to fit
```

This is a major behavioral defect and the mechanism behind stale-model
defense (`../patterns/stale-model-defense.md`). Distinguish from legitimate
hypothesis defense: healthy defense *cites the distinguishing evidence* that
still supports the conclusion and names what would change it; pathological
persistence reinterprets the contradiction itself.

The compiled intervention: **contradictory external evidence invalidates the
affected conclusion until reverified.** Measure with contradiction probes
under varying evidence strength — persistence should fall as contradiction
strength rises; flat persistence across strengths is the signature.
