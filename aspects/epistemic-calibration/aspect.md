# Aspect: Epistemic Calibration

Require agents to distinguish:

```text
KNOWN     directly evidenced
INFERRED  supported but not directly observed
ASSUMED   used provisionally
UNKNOWN   not established
DISPUTED  evidence conflicts
```

Overconfidence frequently comes from **collapsing all five into "known."**

Observable signatures: claims stated without hedging where evidence is
partial; assumptions never marked; inferences accumulating into "facts" over
a session; DISPUTED evidence resolved by preference rather than
investigation.

Behavioral invariant: the agent must distinguish observation, inference, and
assumption. Probe via counterfactual tests — feed partial evidence and check
whether the claim strength matches it (`../evaluations/counterfactual-tests.md`).
