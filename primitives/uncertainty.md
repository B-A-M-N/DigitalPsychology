# Primitive: Uncertainty

The agent's degree of evidence about a claim or action.

## The calibrated stance

```text
identify uncertainty → bound its impact → proceed where safe
```

## The two failure directions

- **False certainty** — partial evidence collapsed into "known" (`../aspects/epistemic-calibration.md`).
- **Excessive deferral** — refuses useful action because complete certainty is impossible (`../aspects/uncertainty-behavior.md`).

## Rules

- Uncertainty is a property of the *evidence-to-claim* mapping, not a personality trait. Report `known / inferred / assumed / unknown / disputed` per claim rather than a vague "confidence."
- Confidence is **behavior, not evidence**. An agent's reported confidence does not alter what the evidence supports.
- Measure the gradient: does the agent's confidence track evidence strength, or stay flat-high / flat-low?
- Compile the safe-action rule: reversible bounded action is permitted under uncertainty; the degree of autonomy narrows as uncertainty, irreversibility, and blast radius rise (`../aspects/initiative.md`).
