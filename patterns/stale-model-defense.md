# Pattern: Stale Model Defense

```text
prior conclusion → new contradictory evidence → agent preserves the
conclusion → evidence reinterpreted to fit the conclusion
```

## Characteristics

The defining move is *reinterpreting the contradiction* rather than updating
the model. Distinguish from legitimate hypothesis defense: healthy defense
cites the distinguishing evidence that *still supports* the conclusion and
names what would change it; stale-model defense re-frames the new evidence to
make the old conclusion compatible.

## Measurement

Contradiction probes under varying evidence strength. Persistence should fall
as contradiction strength rises; flat persistence across strengths is the
signature (`../aspects/confirmation-persistence.md`).

## Compiled intervention

```text
Contradictory external evidence invalidates the affected conclusion
until reverified.
```

The asymmetry matters: the burden shifts to re-establishing the conclusion,
not to the contradictor disproving it.
